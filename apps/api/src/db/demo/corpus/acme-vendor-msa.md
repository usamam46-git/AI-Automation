# Master Services Agreement — MSA-2024-11

**Between:** Northwind Operations Ltd ("the Customer")
**And:** Acme Vendor LLC ("the Supplier")
**Supplier record:** SUP-0442 · Supplier rating A−
**Commencement:** 1 January 2025 · **Renews:** 1 January 2027
**Indicative annual value:** USD 180,000.00

---

## 1. Interpretation

"Order" means a purchase order issued by the Customer under this Agreement.
"Deliverables" means the goods and any associated services described in an Order.
"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in
the Customer's place of incorporation.

Where this Agreement conflicts with the Customer's standard purchasing policy,
this Agreement prevails. Where an Order conflicts with this Agreement, this
Agreement prevails except as to quantity, delivery date and delivery location.

## 2. Pricing

The contracted unit price for catalogue item AC-2291-B is **USD 35.00 per
unit**, held firm for the initial term. An Order for 120 units at the contracted
unit price therefore carries a total of USD 4,200.00.

### 2.1 Volume tiers

| Cumulative units in a calendar year | Unit price |
|---|---|
| 0 – 999 | 35.00 |
| 1,000 – 4,999 | 33.25 |
| 5,000 and above | 31.50 |

Tier pricing is applied prospectively from the Order that crosses the threshold.
The Supplier is not required to rebate Orders placed earlier in the year.

### 2.2 Price changes

The Supplier may propose a price change no more than once in any twelve-month
period, and must give the Customer **not less than 90 days' written notice**. A
price change takes effect only for Orders placed after the notice period has
expired. Orders already placed are fulfilled at the price in force when the Order
was issued.

An invoice presenting a price the Customer has not accepted in writing is a
disputed invoice under clause 6 and is not payable until resolved.

## 3. Ordering and delivery

The Customer places Orders in writing. The Supplier acknowledges each Order
within two Business Days, confirming quantity, unit price and delivery date.

Delivery is to the location named in the Order. Title and risk pass to the
Customer on delivery and acceptance, evidenced by a goods receipt.

The Supplier delivers the exact quantity ordered. Over-delivery is not accepted
and may be returned at the Supplier's cost. Under-delivery is treated as a
partial delivery and is invoiced only for the quantity actually received.

## 4. Invoicing

The Supplier invoices only after delivery has been made and a goods receipt
raised. Each invoice must state the Order number to which it relates, the
quantity delivered, the unit price applied, and the invoice's own unique
reference.

An invoice that does not quote a valid Order number will be returned unpaid.

The Supplier issues one invoice per Order. Consolidated invoices covering several
Orders are not accepted, because they cannot be matched against a single goods
receipt.

## 5. Payment terms

Payment terms are **Net 30 days** from the date of a correctly rendered invoice.
For the avoidance of doubt, the 30-day period runs from the invoice date and not
from the date the invoice is received, provided the invoice is delivered to the
Customer within five Business Days of issue.

Where an invoice is disputed in accordance with clause 6, the payment period is
suspended for the disputed portion only. The undisputed portion remains payable
on the original due date.

### 5.1 Late payment

Interest on undisputed sums paid late accrues at 2% per annum above the
prevailing central bank base rate, calculated daily from the due date until
payment. The Supplier waives any contractual right to suspend delivery on account
of late payment of a disputed sum.

## 6. Disputes

The Customer may dispute all or part of an invoice by written notice within
**30 days of the invoice date**, stating the amount disputed and the grounds. A
dispute raised within that window suspends the Customer's obligation to pay the
disputed amount.

The parties will attempt to resolve a dispute in good faith within 20 Business
Days of the notice, escalating to the Customer's Financial Controller and the
Supplier's Commercial Director if unresolved.

An invoice not disputed within the 30-day window is deemed accepted as to amount,
though this does not waive any claim in respect of the Deliverables themselves.

## 7. Warranties and remedies

The Supplier warrants that the Deliverables will conform to the specification in
the Order and will be free from material defect for twelve months from delivery.

The Customer's remedy for non-conforming Deliverables is, at the Customer's
election, replacement or credit. Where credit is given, the Supplier issues a
credit note quoting the original invoice reference; the Customer does not offset
against an unrelated invoice.

## 8. Term and renewal

The initial term runs to 31 December 2026. The Agreement then renews
automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives
written notice of non-renewal not less than **90 days** before the renewal date.
The next renewal date is **1 January 2027**.

The indicative annual value of USD 180,000.00 is an estimate for planning
purposes. It is not a committed volume and the Customer is under no obligation to
place Orders to that value.

## 9. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within 30 days of
written notice. The Customer may terminate for convenience on 60 days' written
notice, in which case the Customer pays for Deliverables already delivered and
for work in progress properly incurred against an accepted Order.

## 10. Limitation of liability

Neither party excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by
negligence, or for fraud. Subject to that, each party's aggregate liability under
this Agreement in any twelve-month period is limited to the total sums paid or
payable by the Customer under this Agreement in that period.

Neither party is liable for loss of profit, loss of anticipated saving, or
indirect or consequential loss.

## 11. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential for the term
and for three years afterwards, and uses it only for the purposes of this
Agreement. Pricing under clause 2 is the Supplier's confidential information.

## 12. Notices

Notices are given in writing to the addresses in Schedule 1, and are deemed
received on delivery if by hand, or on the second Business Day after posting.
Email notice is effective for disputes under clause 6 but not for termination
under clause 9.
