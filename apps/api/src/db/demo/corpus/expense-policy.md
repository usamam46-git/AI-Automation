# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Northwind Operations Ltd — Finance**
Document reference: FIN-EXP-002
Version 4.1 · Effective 1 March 2026 · Owner: Group Financial Controller

---

## 1. Principle

The company reimburses expenses that are necessarily incurred in the course of
work, that are reasonable in the circumstances, and that are evidenced. An
expense that fails any one of those three tests is not reimbursed, however small.

Employees are expected to spend the company's money as carefully as they would
spend their own, and to choose the reasonable option rather than the cheapest
available one. A policy cannot anticipate every situation; where this document is
silent, ask Finance before incurring the cost rather than afterwards.

## 2. Submission and deadlines

Claims are submitted through the expense system within **30 days of the date the
expense was incurred**. A claim submitted between 31 and 60 days requires the
approval of the claimant's line manager with a written explanation of the delay.

**A claim submitted more than 60 days after the expense was incurred is not
reimbursed.** This is a hard limit driven by the tax treatment of late claims and
is not waived by a manager's approval.

Each claim covers a single calendar month. Claims spanning several months are
returned for resubmission.

## 3. Receipts and evidence

An itemised receipt is required for **every expense of USD 25.00 or more**. A
card statement line is not a receipt: it shows the amount but not what was
purchased, and it is not accepted as evidence on its own.

Below USD 25.00, a receipt is preferred but a description of the expense with the
date, the amount and the business purpose is sufficient.

For meals with external attendees, the claim must name the attendees and their
organisation. A claim for hospitality that does not name the guests is treated as
a personal meal and is not reimbursed.

## 4. Approval thresholds

| Claim total (USD) | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to and including 250.00 | Line manager, routine. |
| Above 250.00 and up to 2,500.00 | Line manager, with itemised review of every line. |
| Above 2,500.00 | Line manager and Finance. |

A claim containing any line that breaches this policy requires Finance review
regardless of the claim total, even where the total is small. The breach, not the
amount, is what triggers the review.

## 5. Travel

### 5.1 Booking

Flights and rail are booked through the company travel desk wherever a suitable
option exists. Self-booked travel is reimbursed at the lower of the amount paid
and the fare the travel desk could have obtained for the same journey booked at
the same time.

Journeys are booked at least **14 days in advance** where the date is known.
Bookings made inside 14 days require a note explaining why.

### 5.2 Class of travel

Economy class for flights under six hours. Premium economy is permitted for
flights of six hours or more, or where the traveller works on the day of arrival.
Business class requires Director approval in advance, in writing.

Standard class for rail, except where a first-class advance fare is cheaper than
the standard-class fare for the same train.

### 5.3 Mileage

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at **USD 0.58 per mile** for the first
10,000 miles in a tax year and USD 0.24 per mile thereafter. Mileage claims must
state the start point, the destination and the business purpose. Commuting
between home and a normal place of work is never reimbursed.

### 5.4 Ground transport

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed where public transport is impractical,
where the traveller is carrying equipment, or where the journey is outside
06:00–22:00 local time. Otherwise public transport is expected.

## 6. Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to the following nightly rates, excluding
tax:

| Location | Nightly cap (USD) |
|---|---|
| London, New York, San Francisco, Tokyo, Zurich | 320.00 |
| Other major cities | 240.00 |
| Elsewhere | 180.00 |

A night above the cap requires a written explanation and line manager approval.
Room service, minibar, hotel gym fees and in-room entertainment are not
reimbursed.

## 7. Meals and subsistence

Meals while travelling on business are reimbursed against receipts up to a daily
cap of **USD 75.00** per person, covering all meals in that day. The cap is a
ceiling, not an allowance: only what was actually spent and evidenced is
reimbursed.

Alcohol is reimbursed only as part of a meal with external attendees, and is
capped at USD 20.00 per attendee. Alcohol claimed on a solo meal is not
reimbursed.

## 8. Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed, regardless of amount or approval:

- Traffic fines, parking fines and congestion charges incurred through a penalty.
- Personal entertainment, including films, streaming and events not attended with
  a client or a candidate.
- Travel insurance purchased separately, as the company holds a group policy.
- Airline lounge access and seat upgrades not covered by clause 5.2.
- Personal grooming, clothing and luggage.
- Loyalty scheme fees, and any cost incurred to earn personal loyalty points.
- Expenses incurred by a spouse, partner or family member accompanying a
  traveller.

## 9. Foreign currency

Expenses in a foreign currency are converted at the rate actually charged by the
employee's card provider, evidenced by the card statement. Where the employee
paid in cash, the mid-market rate on the date of the expense is used.

Conversion fees charged by a card provider are reimbursed. Cash withdrawal fees
are not, because a card payment was available.

## 10. Corporate cards

A corporate card is issued for business expenditure only. Personal use, even
where reimbursed promptly, is a breach of the cardholder agreement.

Corporate card transactions must still be claimed and evidenced through the
expense system within the same 30-day window. An unreconciled corporate card
transaction older than 60 days is recovered through payroll.

## 11. Account coding

Employee travel and subsistence is coded to account **6400**. The offsetting
credit for a reimbursement is the employee payables control account. Expenses
recharged to a client are coded to the project rather than to 6400, and must
carry the project reference on the claim line.

## 12. Enforcement

A claim found to contain a knowingly false line is a disciplinary matter, and the
whole claim is rejected rather than the offending line alone. Repeated
inadvertent breaches are addressed with the line manager in the first instance.

Finance reports quarterly to the Audit Committee on claim volumes, exception
rates and the categories most frequently breached.
